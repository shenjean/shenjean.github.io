## Agar plates for bacterial isolation
The Instant Pot can be used for sterilization but can only hold 250 mL bottles.

### Marine Agar 
1.	Weigh out 3.74g BD Difco™ Marine Agar, and add to bottle
2.	Pour 200ml dH2O from cylinder to bottle and keep cap loose
3.	Weigh out 3g agar and add to bottle
5.	Stir to mix on hot plate at 100C with magnet until dissolved
6.	Autoclave for 30 mins to sterilize
7.	Cool down bottle
8.	Pour plates keeping covers half way on until solid, and then close

### LB Agar
1.	Add 250ml dH2O to bottle
2.	Weigh out 10g LB agar
3.	Stir to mix on hot plate at 100C with magnet until dissolved
4.	Autoclave for 30 mins to sterilize
5.	Cool down bottle
6.	Pour plates keeping covers half way on until solid, and then close
