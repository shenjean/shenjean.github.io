## Making glycerol stocks
>Protocol written by Shira Jaye Anteby-Maleh

1.	Create a solution with 50% glycerol and 50% dH2O
2.	For a total of 1 ml (1,000ul) cell culture: 
     * Label all tubes
     * Pipette (using P1000) 400ul 50% glycerol solution into tubes
3.	Pipette 600ul of each culture
4.	Store at -80C
