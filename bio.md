## Shen Jean Lim, Ph.D., Assistant Professor

![Jean](Pics/Jean_Indoor.jpg)

>My research centers on characterizing host-microbe-environment interactions in marine ecosystems. I integrate field, laboratory, and ‘omics approaches to understand the interplay between microbes (bacteria, viruses, and fungi), host health, and environmental health. On-going projects include 1) the interactions of chemosymbiotic lucinid bivalves with their gill symbionts, environmental microorganisms, and surrounding seagrasses; and 2) viruses, bacteria, and phytoplankton associated with <i>Karenia brevis</i> (red tide) blooms in Florida.

>When I'm not in the lab, I enjoy being outdoors. I have been swimming, biking, and running in Florida and participated in local triathlon, half marathon, and other events. I also enjoy hiking and scuba diving.
